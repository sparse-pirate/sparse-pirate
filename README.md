### About Me

I’m a systems and network administrator with hands-on experience managing servers, networks, and security tools.  
I work across both **Windows** and **Linux** environments, focusing on keeping systems reliable, secure, and well-organized.

I enjoy setting up infrastructure, hardening systems against threats, and continuously improving how things run.  
Outside of work, I maintain a personal homelab where I test security tools and self-hosted services.
